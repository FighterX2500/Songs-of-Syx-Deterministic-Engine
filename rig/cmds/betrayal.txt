# SOS-227: a war on an NPC that broke its treaties with the player costs no betrayal; a treaty the player broke first,
# then a war, costs betrayal as in vanilla. wrelations notes each human's stored opinion entries: BETRAYAL is the one.
# Run it with --humans 2 so the second human's row shows the first human's breaks are not its own.
wrelations -1
wrelations -2
# 1. the first NPC: a trade treaty the player made, the NPC's court cancels it, the player declares war at once:
#    no BETRAYAL entry for human 0 after the war (WAR_DECLARATION and AGRESSION are vanilla's, as before)
wnpcstance -1 trade human
wait 5
wnpcstance -1 neutral npc
wait 5
wdealnpc -1 war
wait 5
wrelations -1
# 2. the second NPC: a trade treaty the player made, the player cancels it, then declares war at once: BETRAYAL for
#    human 0 as vanilla gives it (the cancel and the war, both fresh)
wnpcstance -2 trade human
wait 5
wdealnpc -2 cancel
wait 5
wdealnpc -2 war
wait 5
wrelations -2
