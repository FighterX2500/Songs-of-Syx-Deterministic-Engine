# Area-room planning (ADR 0003, SOS-34): copy 1 plans on its own map only, lifted out of the simulation around its
# steps, so the copies stay identical while it plans; Build is one command (6007 roomBuild) every copy applies.
# A farm without furniture
plan FARM_GRAIN 6 6
build
# A workshop with furniture, indoors: auto walls and a doorway
plan WORKSHOP_SMITHY 5 5
plan-items 2
plan-door
build
# A house, indoors
plan _HOME 4 4
plan-items 1
plan-door
build
# A plan given up: nothing reaches the other copies
plan FARM_VEG 5 5
plan-cancel
# The meeting: a new plan over the built one's corner, made in the same frame as Build, so the built plan's command
# lands while it is local; its overlapping tiles are dropped, the rest builds
plan FARM_GRAIN 4 4
build-plan FARM_VEG 6 6 over
build
# The meeting apart: the local plan is rebuilt whole after the command, then furnished and built
plan FARM_GRAIN 4 4
build-plan WORKSHOP_SMITHY 5 5 apart
plan-items 1
plan-door
build
