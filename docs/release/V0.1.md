# SOSDET V0.1

The first full build of the engine (plan 0002, decision 6). A city and its world map run in lockstep: the same save, seed and players' actions give the same game on every PC. V0.1 is for the owner's 30-minute session. Three game copies run on one PC through the test rig, standing in for three players; the network is the mod's work (SOSGSGMP).

V0.1 is co-op. Every PC plays the same save and the same player faction, and any player's action counts for all.

## Since the first session (SOS-36)

- **Fixed: the siege prompt froze the game.** Auto, Wait and Lift did nothing, because the prompt stopped this PC's game time, so the click's command never reached its step. Prompts the step opens now keep game time running in a session; the world map still stops while one is open.
- **New: the room copier, the mass copier and saved blueprints work.** Each click is one command. A blueprint's contents travel inside the command, so the other PCs don't need your blueprint files.
- **Fixed: messages open in your window** (yearly report, events). The hidden copies still close theirs.
- **Fixed: region buildings** are refused in the step when they would leave a build point negative.
- **Fixed, from the competitor mod's two-PC desync report:** a command could read a cache that only one PC's UI had filled.
- **Resume your saved session:** `rig\play-v01.bat V01B --save SOSDET-V01A-siege.save`.

## Starting the 30-minute session

You need what the build needs (README, "Building"), plus Python 3 and the save `Mogus-72-19f86f1e048-47002c-0-121c.save` in your saves folder. From `C:\ClaudeFolder\SOSDET`:

```
rig\play-v01.bat
```

The script:
1. builds the engine and the rig;
2. starts three copies of the game on the Mogus-72 save, with no invasion;
3. opens copy 1 in a normal window at 1x. That copy is the host, and you play it.

The other two copies run small and off-screen, and replay everything you do at the same steps.

- **Optional arguments:** `rig\play-v01.bat V01B` names the run (default `V01A`). Arguments after the name go to `rig\rig.py`, for example `rig\play-v01.bat V01B --save Other.save`.
- **Ending:** close your window. The other copies are held and compared up to the last step yours reached.
- **When the copies split:** at the first difference every copy is held, your window included.
- **The result:** in `build\rig\runs\<name>\`. `summary.txt` says whether the copies stayed identical; `desync-c<k>.txt` is each copy's desync report if they split.

## What works

- **Speed:** each PC sets its own pace; the step length follows the agreed speed.
- **The city:** laws, decrees, technology, immigration, employment, military orders, names, and room tools: build, remove, refurnish, plan before Build, the room copier, the mass copier and saved blueprints. About 115 commands.
- **The world map:** it runs in the step on every PC.
  - **Armies:** move, stop, intercept, besiege, raid, disband, recruit, hire mercenaries, create armies, and divisions (order, banner, reassign, targets, artillery).
  - **Regions:** autonomy, abandon, name, edicts, squeeze, buildings, attack.
- **Diplomacy:** deals and proposals, vassal release, request expiry, emissaries.
- **Events:** event choices, raider demands and allies, the killer's suspect.
- **World battles:**
  - when a battle comes up, the prompt opens on every PC; auto-resolve or retreat;
  - the battle result: captives, spoils and mercy;
  - a captured settlement: occupy, abandon or puppet.

  While a prompt is open the world map stops on every PC and the city goes on. In vanilla the whole game pauses instead.
- **The simulation's yes/no questions:** "declare war and besiege?" and "take the routed invaders prisoner?".
- **Battles:** field battles, with your orders and the AI general on the host.
- **Engage (co-op):** a world battle fought on the battle map on every PC. Deployment waits for Start, which any player may click; Retreat and the victory or defeat panel's Close end it on every PC, and the game goes on from the save before the battle at the same step.
- **Saves:** the game's own saves (menu, quick save) work during a session and leave your unbuilt room plans out.

## What is blocked in a session

It shows a notice instead:
- **Engage with own cities:** a world battle fought on the battle map works in co-op (SOS-32), not with own cities yet. Auto-resolve or retreat instead.
- **Restarting a battle:** the battle panel's restart.

## Known differences and gaps

- **Slowing down takes about 2 s:** a speed change applies 30 steps after it is taken, and above 1x a step is 1/16 s.
- **Title bonuses:** in a session each title is worth the base 0.5. Vanilla counts the races your profile has won with, which differs per PC.
- **A desync while a world-battle prompt is open:** a resync then leaves the reloading PC without the prompt. The session holds at the first desync, so V0.1 doesn't resync.
- **Not run in a game yet:** world battles and the end of an invasion. No rig run reached them. Please try one.

## What to try, and what to report

Play the city and the world map for 30 minutes as you normally would: build, change laws, move an army, trade or make a deal with a faction, and take a world battle (auto-resolve) if one comes. Then report:
- the run name and what `summary.txt` says;
- if the copies split: roughly what you were doing just before, and the `desync-c<k>.txt` files;
- anything that behaved differently from the plain game, beyond what this page lists.
